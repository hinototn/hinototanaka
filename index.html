Mapping the Unseen

I’m a geography student at Middlebury College working with cartography, GIS, and spatial research. I’ve mapped U.S. military bases in Okinawa, collective housing in Copenhagen, and voting access in Richmond.

I’m interested in visual storytelling and the choices behind a good map. Feel free to reach out on LinkedIn or by email.
