About

I’m Hinoto Tanaka, a geography student at Middlebury College in Vermont. I was born in the U.S. and grew up in Detroit, Nagoya, Suzhou, Hong Kong, Tokyo, and Fishers, Indiana. Moving between places shaped how I see the world, though it wasn’t until I met Professor Jeff Howarth at Middlebury that I found my way to geography.

Jeff taught me that every choice on a map matters, from the smallest label to what we choose to leave out. He taught me to find joy in the details, question the obvious, and care about doing things well. I carry those lessons with me.

Outside of geography, I love drawing, cooking, climbing, and running. I almost always have a sketchbook with me, just in case.

Feel free to reach out via LinkedIn or email.
