Green Spaces by the Border

A map of the largely undeveloped land around Pownal, in the southwest corner of Vermont.

I mapped large parcels with very little impervious surface, along with roads, towns, and neighboring state boundaries.

The small globe and legend help place this local map in a larger geographic context.
