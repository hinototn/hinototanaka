Green Spaces by the Border

A map of the largely undeveloped land around Pownal, in the southwest corner of Vermont.

At a glance

Year
2026
Data
Land parcels, impervious surface, roads, town and state boundaries
Output
A town map with locator globe and inset
Skills
Parcel analysis, layout, locator design

Pownal sits where Vermont meets New York and Massachusetts, and most of it is still green. I mapped which large parcels have almost no pavement or buildings.

How it was made

  1. Large parcels, little pavement
  2. Where in the world is Pownal?

Behind the map

01

Large parcels, little pavement

I mapped large parcels with very little impervious surface, together with roads, towns and the neighbouring state boundaries. Shades of green carry the main message; everything else stays grey.

02

Where in the world is Pownal?

A small town map needs help placing itself. A globe and a small inset take the reader from the planet to the corner of Vermont in two steps, without stealing space from the town.
