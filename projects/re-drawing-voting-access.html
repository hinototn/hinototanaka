Re-Drawing Voting Access

Could Richmond make polling places easier to reach by moving them?

I used QGIS to map the share of residential addresses more than one mile from their polling station. I also looked at households without cars, since distance matters differently when you cannot drive.

I tested alternative polling locations and compared the results. Access improved in 40 precincts but worsened in 15. The average share of addresses more than a mile away fell from 22.9% to 21.2%. Moving a polling place can help, but not everywhere.
