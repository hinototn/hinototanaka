Redlined Chicago

I wanted to see how race, rent, redlining, and green-space access overlap in Chicago.

I used R to prepare census data and QGIS to map the patterns by tract. I also compared the results with two classic models of urban geography.

Higher rents and predominantly White neighborhoods extend north of downtown. Many Black- and Latino-majority tracts to the south and west have lower rents, higher redlining scores, and less access to green space. The patterns show why Chicago cannot be explained by distance from downtown alone.
